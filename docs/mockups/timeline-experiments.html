3 · Story time × reading time

Across: where you read it. Up: when it happened (log-compressed). Events in the story ride the bright ridge; recounted events hang below it, tethered to the moment they're told. Long tethers = deep flashbacks, legends and visions.

4 · The night sky

The centre is now (the latest event in the story). Distance from the centre is time before now, on log rings. Each book claims a slice of sky. Stars are events (brighter = more participants); recounted events are hazy nebulae; relative dates (“1 year after…”) are drawn as constellation lines.
