Relationship graph: design mockup (static)

Nodes are entries, colored by type, sized by how connected they are. Grey lines = shared events (thicker = more). Gold dashed lines = relationships you add by hand (ally, member of, owns…). Tap a line to see why it exists; tap a node to highlight its neighbours. Drag to pan, pinch/scroll to zoom. Events themselves are hidden by default (they're what makes the lines).
